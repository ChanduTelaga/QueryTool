🔒 Every hostname is checked automatically against all suffixes in your FQDN Registry — a pasted list with hosts on different domains is handled correctly in one go, no need to pick just one. Matching hosts get 4 slots (short-upper, short-lower, FQDN-lower, FQDN-upper) as a baseline, 5 if the full FQDN's casing doesn't match either the all-upper or all-lower form, or 6 if the hostname itself is mixed-case too. Using domains that aren't registered yet? Type them above, separated by commas — e.g. .corp.local.com, .example.local.com — to include all of them for this batch, or add them to the FQDN Registry to make them permanent.
